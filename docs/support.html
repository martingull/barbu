Support

For help, bug reports, or questions about Barbu, email martin@insilicoveritas.org.

Reporting a Problem

Please include:

  • Your phone model and iOS or Android version.
  • The Barbu version, if known.
  • The game and steps that led to the problem, and what you expected to happen.
  • A screenshot, if useful. Remove personal information before sending it.

Saved hands and progress are stored on your device. Avoid uninstalling Barbu or clearing its storage while troubleshooting, as that can remove your progress.

You can also report bugs through our GitHub issue tracker. Issues are public, so do not include personal information there.
