# Barbu Card Back

`public/cards/barbu-back.webp` is a 500 x 726 WebP used by the shared
`CardBack.svelte` component. Gin Rummy uses it for the stock pile. It replaces
the small square app icon on a plain rectangle, not the app icon itself.
Neither `public/cards/cards/` nor `public/cards/PNG-cards-1.3/` contained a back.

Generated September 25, 2026 with the built-in image-generation tool. The input
character reference was `src-tauri/icons/128x128@2x.png`. Original generations
remain in Codex's generated-image directory. The selected second generation was
resized with Sharp to match the existing card assets and encoded as WebP at
quality 90. No external artwork was downloaded.

## Initial Prompt

Use case: logo-brand. Asset type: production playing-card BACK bitmap for the
Barbu card game. Create ONE flat, straight-on portrait card-back design,
approximately 1024 by 1488 pixels, ratio 500:726. The entire canvas is the card,
edge to edge, no surrounding scene, no perspective, no drop shadow, no mockup.
The supplied image is a character/style reference only: the distinctive
moustached crowned Barbu king. Reinterpret him as elegant two-way playing-card
line art, two mirrored king busts joined at the waist and rotated 180 degrees,
not a square app icon pasted onto a card. Restrained burgundy field, ivory and
muted gold engraving, small red accents, matching the existing traditional
illustrated king. Strong clear silhouette that remains recognizable on a
45-pixel-wide card. Thin ivory outer border, tasteful paired gold inset rules,
symmetrical restrained corner flourishes and a fine subtle repeated diamond
lattice behind the central king emblem. Professional club-deck aesthetic, clean
crisp illustrated print, balanced generous margins. No words, no letters, no
numerals, no rank or suit corner indices. No gradients, no lighting effects, no
photoreal texture, no extraneous objects. The rectangular artwork must fill the
canvas exactly; the UI will round the corners.

## Refinement Prompt

Refine this production card-back bitmap. Keep the exact two-way mirrored crowned
Barbu king illustration, its face and costume, proportions, placement, the gold
inset rules, ivory outside border and corner flourishes. Change ONLY the brown
textured field behind the figure and its edge lighting into a perfectly flat
rich burgundy #672b43 with a very subtle, uniform fine gold diamond lattice. No
orange, no brown, no patina, no grain, no glow, no vignette, no gradient. The ivory
outside border is flat ivory, not illuminated. Crisp traditional illustrated
playing-card print, not a photograph. Rectangular portrait canvas entirely
occupied by the card. No text, no rank indices, no external background. Keep the
design symmetrical and readable when downscaled to 45 pixels wide.
