Barbu Privacy Policy

Updated: November 18, 2026

Barbu is a card-game learning and practice table. This privacy policy explains how Barbu treats user and device data.

Data Barbu Stores

Barbu stores game progress, saved hands, practice seeds, and table state on your device so you can continue learning and playing. This information stays on your device.

Barbu does not require an account. It does not request your contacts, photos or location, and it does not collect advertising identifiers. If you contact support, we receive your email address and the information you choose to send. Google Play handles payment details; Barbu does not receive your full payment-card or bank-account details.

Data Collection and Sharing

Gameplay and saved progress are not uploaded to a Barbu server. Barbu does not sell user data, use advertising SDKs or track you across other companies' applications. Configured Android versions use RevenueCat for purchase verification and subscription reporting, as described below. This is separate from tracking gameplay or lesson decisions.

Versions without a configured purchase service do not initialize RevenueCat. In versions with it configured, the service can process data when Barbu checks access or loads offers, even if you do not buy a subscription. No advertising attribution integrations are enabled.

Purchases and Subscription Data

In Android versions with purchases configured, Barbu uses Google Play for checkout and RevenueCat to verify purchases, restore access and report subscription status. This processing includes a randomly generated app-user identifier, purchase tokens and transaction history, product identifiers, renewal and expiry information, and technical context such as device/operating-system version, locale and currency. Network requests also expose your IP address to the receiving service. Purchase data is sent over encrypted connections and is not limited to temporary processing on your device.

We use this information to deliver and manage your subscription, resolve purchase issues, prevent misuse and understand subscription performance. We do not send your saved hands, cards or learning progress to RevenueCat. Automatic device-identifier collection and optional SDK diagnostics are disabled. Google and RevenueCat may process information outside your country, including in the United States. See Google's privacy policy and RevenueCat's privacy information.

Processing needed to provide and manage a purchase supports performance of your subscription contract. Security, troubleshooting and subscription reporting support our legitimate interests in operating a reliable service; legal obligations may also require retention of transaction records. Contact us with questions or to exercise applicable privacy rights.

Optional Sharing and External Links

In Android versions with purchases configured, Contact support opens an email draft addressed to Barbu support with your anonymous billing reference and installed Barbu version. Nothing is sent until you choose to send the email. Copy billing reference places only that reference on your device clipboard. Share it privately, not in public bug reports. Neither action includes saved games or payment-card details.

Share Barbu shares only a public Google Play link and a short description through a service you choose, or copies the link to your clipboard. It does not share saved games or learning progress and does not give Barbu access to your contacts.

Get out and play opens an external Google Maps search for card clubs. Barbu does not request or send your location. Google Maps may use its own location permissions and account settings to provide results. External stores, maps, and sharing services follow their own privacy policies.

Crash and Store Diagnostics

Apple, Google Play, or the operating system may provide crash, install, and device diagnostics through developer tools. These diagnostics are used only to understand reliability issues and improve Barbu.

Data Retention and Deletion

Local progress and saved game data remain on your device until you delete Barbu, clear Barbu's storage, or reset your device data.

Uninstalling does not delete purchase records held by Google Play or RevenueCat, or cancel your subscription. Purchase and support records are retained as needed to manage access, resolve disputes and meet legal obligations. To request access, correction or deletion of purchase-related data, email martin@insilicoveritas.org. Include your billing reference from Premium > Purchase support if available. We may also need a Google Play order reference to locate the record and verify the request; do not send full payment-card details. We will arrange deletion where applicable and explain any records that must be retained. Google controls its own store records.

Depending on your location, you may also have rights to portability, restriction or objection to processing, and to complain to your data-protection authority. Deletion of purchase-verification data can affect Premium access; it does not itself cancel Google Play billing. Saved games are not erased when Premium expires.

Children

Barbu is not specifically directed to children under 13. If this changes, this policy and the store privacy declarations will be updated.

Security

Gameplay and progress remain local. Purchase verification uses encrypted connections to Google Play and RevenueCat. No Barbu account or cloud-save service is required.

Changes

This policy may be updated when Barbu changes its features, data practices, or store requirements.
