Barbu Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 20, 2026

Barbu is a card-game learning and practice table. This privacy policy explains how Barbu treats user and device data.

Data Barbu Stores

Barbu stores game progress, saved hands, practice seeds, and table state on your device so you can continue learning and playing. This information stays on your device.

Barbu does not require an account. Barbu does not collect your name, email address, contacts, photos, location, advertising ID, or payment information.

Data Collection and Sharing

Barbu does not send personal data, gameplay data, or saved progress to our servers. Barbu does not sell user data. Barbu does not use advertising SDKs, third-party analytics SDKs, or cross-app tracking.

The Android build may include internet capability as part of the mobile WebView runtime, but Barbu does not use this to transmit personal data.

Crash and Store Diagnostics

Google Play or the operating system may provide crash, install, and device diagnostics through developer tools. These diagnostics are used only to understand reliability issues and improve Barbu.

Data Retention and Deletion

Local progress and saved game data remain on your device until you delete Barbu, clear Barbu's storage, or reset your device data.

Because Barbu does not create user accounts or store personal data on our servers, there is no server-side account data to delete.

Children

Barbu is not specifically directed to children under 13. If this changes, this policy and the Google Play declarations will be updated.

Security

Barbu limits data storage to local gameplay and progress data needed for the card table experience. No personal account data is stored by Barbu servers.

Changes

This policy may be updated when Barbu changes its features, data practices, or store requirements.

Contact

Barbu is developed by Martin Gull. For privacy questions, open an issue at github.com/martingull/barbu/issues.
