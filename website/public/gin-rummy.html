Learn Gin Rummy

When can you knock, and when can you go gin?

Knock: ten or fewer.
Gin: exactly zero.

In classic Gin Rummy, you may knock when the cards left unmatched after your discard total ten points or fewer. Going gin means all ten remaining cards belong to sets or runs: zero deadwood.

Having a legal knock does not mean you must take it. You can keep playing to improve your hand.

What is deadwood?

A meld is a set of three or four cards of the same rank, or a run of at least three consecutive cards in one suit. Each card can belong to only one meld. Cards outside your melds are deadwood.

Reveal the answer

You may knock, but you cannot go gin. The three melds cover nine cards. Only 2♦ remains unmatched, so your deadwood is two points. That is within the knock limit of ten, but not the zero required for gin.

Does a knock guarantee a win?

No. After a knock, your opponent can form melds and lay off unmatched cards onto your exposed melds. If their remaining deadwood equals or beats yours, they undercut you. There are no layoffs against gin.
