How we handle this request

Martin Gullaksen operates Barbu. This page does not track opens or clicks, and the private reference in your link does not contain your email address. Opening the page alone does not unsubscribe you.

When you confirm, the service records an opt-out against a hashed email reference. Hashes are pseudonymous data, not anonymous data. We retain the minimum reference and opt-out date needed to avoid contacting you again. Your private link remains usable; confirming more than once is safe.

Amazon Web Services hosts this outreach service in the United States. Network requests expose your IP address to the provider. The service does not deliberately log your email address, private link, request body or IP address, and it is separate from Barbu gameplay and purchase services.

For questions or requests about outreach records, contact Martin using the email above. See also the Barbu privacy policy.
