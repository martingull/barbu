# Barbu Screen Plan

Current navigation and layout contract, checked October 30, 2026. See
[FEATURES.md](FEATURES.md) for product scope and
[game architecture](docs/game-architecture.md) for component ownership.

## Catalog

The first screen is a usable game collection, not a marketing landing page.
It offers the latest saved game, other saved games, and the Hearts introduction
or its continuation. A completed introduction opens Learn rather than restarting.

The collections are:

- Hearts & changing contracts: Hearts and Barbu.
- Partners & tricks: Whist, Spades and Bridge.
- Rummy games: Gin Rummy.
- Card Skills: Card Counting I.

Collection labels help discovery; they do not replace rules-family metadata.
Planned games are filtered out. Canasta is paused and hidden. Do not expose
subscriber placeholders simply because their metadata exists.

Share Barbu and Get out and play sit below the collections. The first shares the
Google Play listing with copy/select fallback; the second opens a Google Maps
search. Neither creates an account, requests location, or starts multiplayer.

## Table Navigation

Every active table uses **Learn | Play**, generated by the shared table metadata.
Practice belongs within Learn. Pro metadata is retained for future work but its
tab is hidden. Keep navigation consistent even when game mechanics differ.

`App.svelte` routes the catalog, references and game features and provides shared
services. Each `features/<game>/` module owns its interactions; domain modules
own rules and state transitions. Do not rebuild game-local screens in the shell.

## Learn

The learning menu distinguishes four intentions:

- Continue the next lesson in the path.
- Choose a specific skill or its Try cards exercise.
- Play a guided hand with automatic coaching.
- Read reference material when needed.

Do not list the same topic again in a separate Practice tab or prominent mixed
Quick Drill section. Retained generator/internal action names are not navigation.
Card Counting uses the shared shell for memory activities without claiming an
authored course progression that it does not have.

Lessons combine short concepts/examples, card decisions and review. Authored
worked/supported/independent stages reduce support; bounded follow-ups reinforce
mistakes. Shared feedback and read-only decision review explain the actual choice.
Never expose scripted later cards or hidden hands as if visible at decision time.

Retry and Next lesson belong next to each other. Play and Return to Learn sit
below as secondary destinations. Completing a course should lead to guided or
ordinary play, with Reset lessons available for an intentional fresh path.
Leaving an exercise early must not complete it.

## Guided Hands

Hearts, Gin Rummy, Spades, Whist, Bridge and Barbu reuse their normal play engines
in unsaved guided sessions. Show coaching and explanations automatically, without
Show hint or Explain trick toggles. Recaps refer to actual events in that hand.

Guided hands must not overwrite a saved match or award lesson mastery just for
finishing. Replay keeps the original deal. Bridge retains its auction and dummy
rules; Domino retains its lane layout. Game-specific rules belong in adapters,
not branches in the shared learning shell.

## Play

Start or continue a saved local game from its Play menu. Keep match mode, passing,
bidding and settlement with the relevant game; share card selection, review and
result presentation where their contracts match.

- Hearts passes before tricks, including a hold hand in the rotation.
- Whist exposes single-game/rubber choices before play.
- Spades shows the deal before the player locks their bid.
- Bridge auctions into contract play; expose dummy only after the opening lead.
- Gin Rummy uses stock/upcard and discard actions, not trick-taking controls.
- Barbu introduces each contract in its fixed-order training run; Domino uses
  suit lanes instead of trick slots.

Finished matches offer a clear result and new-game action, not another playable
hand in an already completed session. Reference and learning must not replace or
silently reset a saved game.

## Active Table Layout

- Respect safe areas and Android navigation bars. Thumb controls stay reachable.
- Keep the active hand directly above actions, with feedback above the hand.
- Reserve feedback, hand and action space so selection, coaching, completed
  tricks and changing text cannot resize the board between decisions.
- Use shared card sizing and row layout. Keep slots and seat labels stable as
  hands shrink; Bridge dummy/reference cards must not duplicate a selectable hand.
- Keep cards readable rather than solving every collision by shrinking them.
- Use bounded scrolling for long guidance and game-specific large layouts.
  Menus, references and results may scroll; do not globally lock their overflow.
- Check compact phones, wider browser windows and iPad compatibility mode.
  A passing phone screenshot does not establish all-layout correctness.

`TablePlaySurface`, `CardChoiceHand`, shared layout measurement and the table
styles own these constraints. Guided surfaces use reserved feedback space;
ordinary Play should not gain unused guidance boxes.

## Reference And Identity

Reference explains object, players, cards, deal, play, scoring, tactics and named
varieties. Keep internal roadmaps and implementation status out of player copy.
Document training conventions without presenting them as universal club rules.

Use the existing felt/table palette, cream action emphasis and restrained
penalty colors. Barbu and the deck portraits act as concise coaches, not large
decorative obstacles. Keep game artwork, readable suit symbols and accessible
card names consistent across Learn and Play.

## Verification

Use `task ui:test` for the full browser matrix. `task ui:test:tables` is a legacy
focused subset, not complete coverage of every current game or guided surface.
Use the relevant tests under `tests/e2e/` for focused changes and inspect screenshots
at multiple sizes. Browser checks share production gameplay but do not replace
physical-device testing of native safe areas, links, sharing or upgrades.

Future monetization or multiplayer needs a separate product flow. Do not add
empty Pro screens, room setup or entitlement checks inside lessons and tables.
